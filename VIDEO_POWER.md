# 0.8.1 video power experiment
The Video Eco option (default on) asks Android for a supported approximately 60 Hz refresh rate at the existing resolution in browser fullscreen and local media playback. The OS may ignore this preference. Restore the previous window settings on exit. GeckoView suspends inactive session media, and the main activity deactivates sessions on stop, reactivating the selected one on start. The local player retains hardware decoding when supported, with software fallback for other formats. Hidden playback controls no longer poll position. The app does not cap a site's actual video resolution or frame rate and does not change CPU/GPU frequencies. Coil whine is hardware dependent and cannot be guaranteed fixed. Compare the same video at identical resolution, frame rate, brightness and charging state, with Video Eco on/off. Check full-screen transitions, tab switching, returning from background, and local audio/video. No Tor implementation changes in this patch. Stable branch and signing identity remain unchanged.
