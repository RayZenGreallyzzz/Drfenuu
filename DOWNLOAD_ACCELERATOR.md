# 0.8.6 Download Accelerator — stage 1
Existing GeckoView WebResponse bodies continue to supply authenticated, blob, and ordinary downloads. No replacement HTTP stack or replay of GET/POST requests; no cookie or URL export. Queue defaults to 2 parallel workers (1–3 configurable), up to 12 unfinished tasks. Active paused tasks retain their slot and socket; a paused queued task is skipped. Pausing waits between read/write chunks; it is not persistent Range resume and a server can time out. Cancellation closes response body and incomplete destinations are removed. Existing MediaStore/legacy output remains; known identity body length is checked before publication. Activity destruction cancels incomplete jobs (no foreground service or persistent queue yet). The download panel updates existing cards, samples speed, and shows approximate remaining time; no parallel segmented requests. Full HTTP Range resume across network failures and process death, multi-segment download and notifications are follow-up work, not implemented. Test authenticated downloads, blob responses, queue order, pauses/cancel, unknown lengths, changing limits, MediaStore cleanup and activity shutdown on a device. No new dependencies, permissions, or signing changes.
