# 0.8.2 video performance settings
Settings → Video and performance exposes separate persisted preferences for approximate 60 Hz fullscreen video, approximate 60 Hz across the entire browser, background media suspension, and LibVLC hardware decoder priority. Existing videoEco preference is preserved; background suspension defaults to the old videoEco value until changed. The display request uses only supported modes at the current resolution and is advisory. Hardware priority applies to local media opened in the media center, not web video; disabling it restores standard hardware decoding with fallback. Local diagnostic enumeration is on demand and does not initialize decoders, change the network, or benchmark the device. No codec is forcibly disabled in GeckoView; site quality remains controlled by its player. The Android-reported hardware flag does not prove active hardware decoding or performance. Test switching all options, cold/warm lifecycle, fullscreen exit, local media and diagnostics on the user's Android 15 tablet. Coil whine improvement is not guaranteed.
